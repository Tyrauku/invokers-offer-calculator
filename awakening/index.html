How it works. Costs are the measured node totals of every awakening star on one unit per rarity; Soul Signs change which stats the nodes give, not what they cost. Dungeon stages drop one essence or crest tile per battle, so a stage's expected haul is the sum of amount × chance from its Rewards panel. Spare lower-tier items are traded up at the Item Trader, 10 for 1. Faction Rune rates come from two long auto-battle runs, one Hard and one Insane, and will vary by stage. Gems assume all the energy is bought in one day with refills (the first 100 energy costs 50 gems, the second 100 costs 100, every 100 after that 150) and Multi-Battle Tokens at 40 for 100 gems; VIP bonuses and event energy are not counted.
